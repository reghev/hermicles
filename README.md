Hermicles

This is one of my projects for beest, its an esp32-c3-mini-1-n4 going to an LSM6DS3TR Gyroscope with a lithium ion battery, that sits in my suitcase when travelling and connects to my phone over bluetooth and shows me what angle its at so i can see if its flipped over or diagonal or something
